Same story, different woman?

You meet someone. The start is exciting. Then the same problems show up, the same arguments, the same ending. You tell yourself she was the problem. Until it happens again.

It is not bad luck. It is a pattern: who you are drawn to, what you tolerate, and how you show up once you are in. And you cannot change a pattern you cannot see.

This workbook makes your pattern visible, then walks you through changing it, one honest exercise at a time. By the end you will know why it keeps happening and exactly what you will do differently.

What you will do inside

  • Map your last four relationships and find the thread they all share.
  • Tell real attraction from anxiety that feels like butterflies.
  • Set your standards and dealbreakers before you are emotionally invested.
  • Spot green and red flags early and learn what women notice about you.
  • Find your role in the pattern: Rescuer, Chaser, Pleaser or Avoider.
  • Say the hard thing clearly with word-for-word scripts for tough conversations.
  • Date with a scorecard so consistency, not intensity, decides who gets your time.
  • Follow a 30-day reset plan and keep 10 rules that stick.

Notes from her side of the table
